Free advice: don't switch your advertising off

This client paused his ads when cash was tight, then restarted them. Every time, Google starts over from nothing, and you pay again while it relearns.

On his account: every visitor cost 30% more. Same trade, same city, eight weeks apart. Better to spend less each day than to stop for a month.
