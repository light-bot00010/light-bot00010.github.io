What I do

Google Ads

Exact-match structure, manual bidding, every euro traceable to a specific query. On small budgets that's the only way to know where the money goes — and the first thing I check is that conversions are actually being measured, before optimising anything.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile, service pages, site structure, content that answers what people in your sector actually search for. SEO is slow work: it doesn't produce a result in three weeks, and I'd rather say so before starting than after.
